Curriculum

From first principles to a process of your own.

15 modules, from your first order to a written trading plan. 77 core lessons and 4 real walkthroughs. All available from day one.

Stage 01 · Modules 0–3

Market foundations and your account

Shares, IPOs, NSE and BSE, NIFTY and Sensex, order types, demat setup, charges and who's who in the market.

Know the tools and language of Indian markets

Stage 02 · Modules 4–6

Fundamental analysis

Business models, moats, SWOT and promoters. Annual reports: P&L, balance sheet and cash flow. ROE, ROCE and intrinsic value.

Work through a structured stock evaluation checklist

Stage 03 · Modules 7–10

Technical analysis

Timeframes, trends, support and resistance, candlesticks, chart patterns, and indicators like RSI and MACD, with their limits.

Spot and explain chart patterns on practice charts

Stage 04 · Modules 11–12

Price action and risk

Market structure, breakouts and fake-outs. Position sizing, the 1% rule, stop-loss, reward to risk and drawdowns.

Size a trade so one loss can't sink you

Stage 05 · Modules 13–14

Psychology and your own system

Fear, greed, revenge trading and overconfidence. Pre-trade checklists, a trade journal, backtesting and paper trading.

A written, testable process instead of tips

Do I need to know anything first?
This is Level 2. It helps to have your money basics sorted: a budget, an emergency fund and insurance. If those are new to you, start with Level 1, Personal Finance Mastery.
Do you give stock tips?
No. It teaches you how to study a company, read a chart and manage risk. No buy or sell calls, no personal investment advice, and no course can promise returns.
Do I need a demat account?
No. Nothing in the course needs a live account or real money; the practice exercises and paper-trading lessons work without one. Module 2 walks you through opening an account when you're ready.
