Personal Finance Mastery. It builds the foundations before the stock market.

No. There is no bundle at the moment. Each course is bought separately.

Yes. Every lesson is taught in Tamil with Indian examples. The app and site are in Tamil and English.

As soon as your payment is confirmed. Log in to the Stonkzz School app (Android, iOS or web) with the phone number you paid with. Access is lifetime, including future updates.

Yes, on Android, iPhone or the web. You can use your account on up to 2 devices.

Yes. Score 60/100 or more across the module challenges and the final assessment for a Stonkzz School certificate of completion. It is not a NISM, SEBI or other official qualification.

No. Stonkzz School teaches. It does not give personal investment advice or trading calls.

Free videos answer one question at a time. The course puts every subject in order, with practice and a challenge after each module.

Not yet. Mutual Funds Mastery and Options Trading 101 are coming soon. Live classes and workshops are planned. None are included with a current purchase.
