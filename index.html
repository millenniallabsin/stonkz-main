Financial education should not begin with a mistake.

The first salary lands. The bank suggests a policy. A friend says start a SIP. Someone forwards a stock tip. Most of us learn money by getting one of these wrong. It shouldn’t take a loss to learn.

Every salary asks three questions.

Income

“Where did my salary go?”

Budgets, emergency funds, EMIs and credit cards. Know where every rupee goes before the month ends.

Protection

“What am I actually buying?”

Health cover, life cover and tax. Know what a policy really gives you before you sign.

Investing

“Why am I choosing this?”

SIPs, funds and stocks, picked for a reason that’s yours, not a forwarded tip.

Stonkzz School answers all three, in order, in Tamil.
