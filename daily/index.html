FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Stonkzz Daily?

A pre-market report on the Indian stock market, laid out like a morning newspaper. Around 11 pages cover NIFTY levels, the mood index, options data, FII/DII flows, global cues, gold and silver, and the day's important news.

Who is it for?

Investors, traders and anyone learning about the market who wants a clear picture every morning without checking ten apps. Beginners are welcome; the report is written in plain language.

When and where do I get the report?

By 8:00 AM IST on every trading day, in our private subscriber channel. You get your personal join link right after payment. There is no report on weekends or market holidays.

Do you give stock tips or buy/sell calls?

No. Stonkzz Daily does not give recommendations, tips or investment advice. It presents market data and news so you can make your own decisions.

Where does the data come from?

Market data is gathered from publicly available platforms: NSE, BSE, Moneycontrol, Investing.com, Trendlyne and Sensibull. The sources are listed at the end of every report.

Does my plan renew automatically?

No. You pay once for the period you choose. We send you a reminder before your plan ends, and you can renew in a couple of taps if you want to continue.

What happens when my plan ends?
